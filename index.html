Shared budgets, made simple

CashFall helps you and the people you share money with keep one clear picture of income and spending. Track entries, set budgets, and see where the month is going.

What you can do

  • Share a budget with family or a partner and keep it in sync.
  • Scan a receipt or import a photo or PDF. The text is read on your device.
  • Export your entries: CSV and JSON are free, PDF and Excel come with Pro.
  • Try it as a guest first, then create an account when you are ready.
  • Lock the app with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

Available on

iPhone and iPad (free, with ads that Pro removes) and Mac (a separate paid app, no ads).
