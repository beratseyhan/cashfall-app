Who we are

CashFall is a shared household budget app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It is made by Berat Seyhan, an independent developer, who is the data controller. Contact for privacy questions, access, deletion and complaints: berseygames@gmail.com.

In short

You can use CashFall as a guest without an account; guest entries stay on your device. If you create an account, your entries are stored in the cloud so they sync across your devices and can be shared with the people you invite. We do not sell your data, we do not track you across other companies’ apps and websites, and CashFall never asks for your bank login or card number. Receipts are read on your device. The free iPhone and iPad app shows ads; the Mac app is a separate paid app and has no ads. This policy covers CashFall for iPhone, iPad and Mac, version 1.5 and later.

Receipts and the camera

When you scan a receipt or import a photo, image file or PDF, the text is read on your device using Apple’s Vision framework. The image is not uploaded to any server for reading. Only the entry you choose to save (amount, merchant, date and so on) is stored like any other entry. The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to scan or import, and you can revoke access in iOS Settings.

Advertising and consent

  • The free iPhone and iPad app shows ads: a banner on some screens, an occasional full-screen ad, and optional rewarded videos that unlock one PDF or Excel export. There are no ads while you add or edit an entry.
  • CashFall Pro removes all ads. People who installed CashFall before version 1.5 (Founders) never see ads.
  • The Mac app is a separate paid app. It shows no ads and has no ad SDK.
  • Where the law requires it (for example the EEA, UK and Türkiye) a consent screen from Google UMP is shown. Ads and analytics follow your choice. You can change it later in Settings → “Privacy choices”.
  • Without consent, ads (where shown) are non-personalised and analytics stays off.
  • You can also limit ad tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. CashFall does not ask for the tracking permission.

Guest mode and shared budgets

  • Guest mode: up to 50 entries are kept on your device without an account. When you sign up they are copied into a new project in your account and then removed from the local store once the upload succeeded.
  • Shared budgets: everyone you invite to a project can see the project’s entries and the display name, e-mail address and role of the other members. Owners and admins can change roles and remove members. Share a budget only with people you trust.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Version 1.5 sends no remote push notifications.
  • App Lock: Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode can lock CashFall when it opens. This works entirely on your device; we receive no biometric data.

How long we keep data

  • Account data and entries: until you delete the account or the project.
  • When you delete an entry, a small marker (entry ID, month and who deleted it) remains so other devices can remove it too. Markers are removed when the project is deleted.
  • Analytics events and crash reports are kept by Google for the limited retention period configured for the service. Anonymous analytics and crash logs are not removed when you delete your account.
  • Purchase records at Apple and RevenueCat are kept as long as needed for legal, tax and accounting obligations.
  • Backups and logs at our providers may persist for a limited period after deletion and then expire.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Profile → Delete Account. This deletes your sign-in, your user document and your memberships. Projects you own are deleted completely, including for the other members. To keep a shared budget, transfer ownership or export your data first. Sign in with Apple tokens are revoked.
  • Deleting the account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in your Apple Account settings.
  • If you cannot use the app, e-mail berseygames@gmail.com from your account address and we will delete the account within 30 days.
  • Guest data on the device is deleted when you delete the app.

Your rights and your data

Depending on where you live (GDPR in the EEA and UK, KVKK in Türkiye, CCPA/CPRA in California and similar laws) you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent at any time. CSV and JSON export of your entries is free for everyone, inside the app (PDF and Excel export is part of Pro). For any right not covered in the app, write to berseygames@gmail.com. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye, the KVKK Board). California residents: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising as defined by the CCPA.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

Contract (providing the app and your account), legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, crash fixing), consent (analytics and personalised ads) and legal obligation (tax records for purchases).

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to each project is limited by server rules to its active members. You can protect the app with App Lock. No system is perfectly secure.

Children

CashFall is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We will post changes here and update the date below. For material changes we will also tell you in the app.
