Contact us

Write to berseygames@gmail.com. We usually reply within 24 to 48 hours. Please include your device model, iOS or macOS version, the CashFall version and what happened. Never send your password.

How do accounts and sync work?

You can start as a guest (up to 50 entries, stored on your device only). Create an account from the sign-in screen with e-mail, Sign in with Apple or Google, and your guest entries move into a new project in your account. Signed-in entries sync across your devices and with the people you invite to a shared budget. If sync looks stale, check your connection, reopen the app, and make sure you are signed in with the same account on both devices.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile and choose Delete Account. This deletes your sign-in, your data and your memberships. Shared budgets you own are deleted for all their members, so transfer ownership or export first if you want to keep them. It does not cancel an App Store subscription: cancel that in your Apple Account settings. If you cannot open the app, e-mail us from your account address. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I export my data?

CSV and JSON export is free for everyone. PDF and Excel (XLSX) export is part of Pro; on the free iPhone and iPad app you can also unlock a single PDF or Excel export by watching an optional rewarded video.

How do I restore my purchases?

On iPhone and iPad open the Pro screen and choose Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy. The Mac app is a separate paid app and is not unlocked by an iPhone purchase.

How does App Lock work?

In Settings turn on App Lock to require Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode when CashFall opens. It works only on your device; your biometric data is never available to us. If you forget your device passcode, use Apple’s device recovery options.

What is guest mode?

Guest mode lets you try CashFall without an account. You can keep up to 50 entries on your device. They are not synced or backed up by us, so deleting the app deletes them. Create an account at any time to keep them and sync.

Ads and Pro

The free iPhone and iPad app shows ads: a banner on some screens, an occasional full-screen ad and optional rewarded videos. There are no ads while you add or edit an entry. CashFall Pro removes all ads and unlocks unlimited PDF and Excel export. People who installed CashFall before version 1.5 never see ads. The Mac app is a separate paid app and has no ads.

How do I change my privacy choices?

Open Settings and choose “Privacy choices” to review your ad and analytics consent at any time. You can also limit ad tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
